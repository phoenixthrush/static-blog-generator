# A little corner of the internet

This is a **sample journal** for trying out the design. These entries are fictional; your own journal belongs in `content/`.

Some days leave a whole page behind. Others leave a photograph, a song, or just a few lines. This is a place to keep them.

## Have a look around

Open a year in the table of contents, pick a month, and turn to a page. The latest entry has a small illustrated postcard, a sound, and a text attachment to try out the different assets.

> A personal website can be a little room you keep adding things to.

No rush. Stay a while.
